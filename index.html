About Us

M. Lewis Capital Partners Ltd seeks to fill a structural gap in Zambia and African private markets by operating as a boutique Investment Bank and Private Equity firm focused on small-to-mid-cap transactions often overlooked by larger institutions across multiple sectors in Africa.

Through the M. Lewis Group umbrella, we operate a platform of specialized divisions that function as integrated business units — not separate legal entities — enabling focused execution across investment banking, private equity, real estate, industrial operations, innovation, and commodities.

We are evolving how investment and advisory solutions are delivered across Africa by combining sector-specific expertise with hands-on execution, disciplined capital allocation, and long-term value creation.
