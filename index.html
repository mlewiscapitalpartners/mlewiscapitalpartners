About Us

M. Lewis Capital Partners Ltd is a boutique investment banking and private equity firm focused on small-to-mid-cap transactions across African markets.

Through the M. Lewis Group platform, we integrate specialized execution capabilities across sectors including advisory, private equity, real estate, industrial operations, innovation, and commodities.

These are not separate legal entities, but integrated business units designed to originate, structure, and execute high-impact investments.
