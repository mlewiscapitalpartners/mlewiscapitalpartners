About M. Lewis Group

M. Lewis Group is an Africa-focused, client-led, digitally-enabled finance and investments brand headquartered in Lusaka, Zambia. We operate four divisions: Boutique Investment Banking, Private Equity & Venture Capital, Real Estate Investment, and Financial Inclusion & Education. Our focus is on sub-$30M transactions across Media, Technology, Energy, FinTech, and Local Services.

BOUTIQUE INVESTMENT BANKING

M. Lewis Investment Banking

Sub-$30M M&A • Capital Raising • Restructuring

M. Lewis Investment Banking provides boutique corporate finance advisory for mid-market African businesses. We focus on transactions below $30 million, where personalized attention and local knowledge matter most. Target sectors: Media & Content, Technology & Software, Energy & Power, FinTech & Financial Inclusion, and Local Services Businesses.

PRIVATE EQUITY & VENTURE CAPITAL

M. Lewis Capital Partners

Patient capital for Africa's high-growth businesses

M. Lewis Capital Partners invests $250,000 to $10 million in growth-stage and early-stage businesses across Africa. Focus sectors: Media & Content, Technology & Software, Energy & Power, FinTech & Financial Inclusion, and Local Services. We take board seats, provide operational support, and prepare companies for exit within 5-7 years.

REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT

M. Lewis Real Estate

Income-producing property for Africa's growing economy

M. Lewis Real Estate acquires, develops, and manages income-producing properties valued at $500,000 to $5 million. Focus: logistics warehousing, Grade A offices for tech and media companies, affordable housing, and student accommodation. Target net yields: 10-14%, total IRR: 15-20%.

FINANCIAL INCLUSION & EDUCATION

M. Lewis Institute

Practical finance education for African professionals

M. Lewis Institute provides practical, accessible financial training to corporate professionals, SME owners, and retail learners. Courses include Finance for Non-Finance Managers, SME Financial Literacy, Introduction to Investing, and Certificate in SADC Cross-Border Investment. Revenue subsidises free community workshops in Lusaka.
