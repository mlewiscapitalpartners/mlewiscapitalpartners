M. Lewis Capital Partners

Investment Banking for Africa's Growth Markets

Private Markets Advisory for Founders & Investors. Trusted Partnerships. African Opportunity.

Overview

M. Lewis Capital Partners is a boutique investment banking and private markets advisory firm focused on supporting founders, growth businesses, investors, and institutions across Africa.

We specialize in transactions below US$30 million, providing hands-on advisory and execution support for businesses and investors seeking flexible, relationship-driven solutions in emerging and frontier markets.

As part of M. Lewis Group, we combine entrepreneurial insight, strategic partnerships, and cross-border networks to help clients raise capital, execute transactions, and unlock long-term value across Sub-Saharan Africa.

What We Do

Private Placements

We help businesses raise growth capital through private market transactions, connecting companies with strategic investors, private equity firms, venture capital funds, family offices, and high-net-worth investors.

Private Equity & Venture Capital Advisory

We support investors and founders with deal structuring, fundraising strategy, investment readiness, market access, and transaction execution across African private markets.

Entrepreneurial Finance Advisory

We work closely with entrepreneurs and founder-led businesses on growth strategy, financial structuring, succession planning, liquidity events, and long-term value creation.

Cross-Border Transactions

We facilitate strategic partnerships and transactions between African businesses and international investors seeking opportunities in emerging markets.

AfriLeap Capital

Unlocking Growth for Africa's Entrepreneurs & SMEs | Built Differently, raising private credit and VC vehicles.

Overview

AfriLeap Capital is a founder-led, impact-driven & pre-launch VC platform built differently, raising private credit and VC vehicles for African SMEs.

We are building a hybrid capital platform for African SMEs. Our flagship initiative, MoGrow FlexFund, is a private credit facility designed to provide non-dilutive working capital. We are now raising initial capital to deploy the facility and scale across target markets.

The Gap We Fill

African SMEs need working capital, not just equity. AfriLeap Capital exists to fill that gap — but we cannot do it alone. We are currently seeking private credit partners to launch MoGrow FlexFund, our flagship non-dilutive financing facility.

Beyond capital, AfriLeap integrates technology-driven financial tools and operational advisory services to strengthen SME performance, enhance financial management, and support long-term scalability. Capital alone is insufficient. Execution support is the multiplier.

Our Vehicles

💰 MoGrow FlexFund

Private credit facility providing non-dilutive working capital for revenue-generating SMEs. Focus on trade financing, inventory purchase, and short-term operational needs.

📈 LeapGrowth Fund

Venture capital vehicle for scalable, high-growth SMEs with proven business models seeking expansion capital.

Target Enterprises

Once capitalized, MoGrow FlexFund will provide non-dilutive trade financing to revenue-generating SMEs — with a focus on:

Women-led enterprises Youth-led enterprises Graduate-led enterprises

Partner With Us

AfriLeap Capital is an impact-driven platform structuring two vehicles: MoGrow FlexFund (private credit for SME working capital) and LeapGrowth Fund (venture capital). We are actively seeking private credit partners and impact investors to join us as founding capital partners.
