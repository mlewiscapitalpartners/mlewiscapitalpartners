Who We Are

Strategic Advisory. Private Capital. Long-Term Partnership.

M. LEWIS GROUP is a Zambia-headquartered institutional platform spanning strategic advisory, corporate finance, investment banking, and private capital. The platform is focused on building businesses into growth-ready enterprises, financing their expansion, and scaling their impact across Africa's productive economy.

We combine strategic advisory, private capital, and operating partnerships to help companies strengthen their foundations, become growth-ready, and transition from promising local businesses into scalable regional platforms.

Our focus extends beyond investing in companies. We identify businesses positioned at critical points in Africa's value chains and work alongside them to strengthen the systems required for growth — connecting capital, technology, production, infrastructure, logistics, and markets to build more competitive and scalable enterprises.

INVESTMENT FIRM

AfriLeap Capital

Investing in the missing middle. Building for scale.

Backing growth-ready businesses across Zambia and Southern Africa with capital and strategic support.

As part of the M. LEWIS GROUP portfolio of enterprises, AfriLeap Capital is a Zambia-based investment firm focused on the missing middle of African enterprise.

We back growth-ready businesses seeking approximately US$25,000 to US$5 million in capital — businesses that are often too large for microfinance and traditional startup support, yet too early, too small or too risky for conventional institutional finance.

Our focus is on businesses with strong founders, proven or emerging market demand, sound commercial fundamentals and the potential to scale.

But capital alone is rarely enough. AfriLeap partners with founders to strengthen the businesses behind the investment — providing strategic support, financial discipline, governance, networks and access to follow-on capital.

We exist to help promising businesses cross the gap from potential to scale.

Investment Criteria

  • Strong Founders – Founders with a clear vision, resilience, and the ability to execute
  • Market Demand – Proven or emerging market demand with clear growth potential
  • Commercial Fundamentals – Sound financials, clear unit economics, and a viable business model
  • Scalable Potential – Businesses with the potential to scale across markets and sectors

Investment Range: US$25,000 – US$5 million

How We Partner

Capital alone is rarely enough. We partner with founders to strengthen the businesses behind the investment by providing:

  • Strategic Support – Business strategy, market positioning, and growth planning
  • Financial Discipline – Financial modelling, budgeting, and operational efficiency
  • Governance – Board advisory, corporate structuring, and oversight
  • Networks – Access to partners, customers, and industry connections
  • Follow-On Capital – Access to larger pools of institutional capital for scaling

Our Mission

We exist to help promising businesses cross the gap from potential to scale.

By investing early, partnering closely and taking a long-term view, AfriLeap aims to build a pipeline of stronger, more scalable African businesses that can ultimately attract larger pools of institutional capital.

Basiwel Ventures is the venture-building and early-stage investment platform of M. LEWIS GROUP, focused on building and backing high-potential businesses that can strengthen Africa's productive economy.

We pursue two complementary pathways: we build ventures around compelling market opportunities, and we back promising early-stage businesses with the potential to become growth-ready enterprises. Geographic Focus

Our geographic focus is Zambia and selective SADC markets, with particular attention to businesses that help the region produce, process, move, finance and distribute more effectively.

We focus on opportunities across:

  • Food and agribusiness
  • Energy
  • Logistics and trade
  • Manufacturing and industrials
  • Technology-enabled infrastructure
