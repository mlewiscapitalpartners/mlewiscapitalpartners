Overview

M. Lewis Capital Partners is an Africa-focused boutique investment firm that combines investment banking expertise with private equity capabilities to support SME capital raising, growth equity, and cross-border transactions across African markets. We bridge the gap between African businesses seeking capital and international investors looking for credible, high-potential opportunities on the continent. We specialize in sub-USD 30 million transactions, with particular focus on founder-led businesses, SME expansion financing, and underserved mid-market opportunities.
