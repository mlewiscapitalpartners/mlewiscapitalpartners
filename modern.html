Boutique Private Equity & Investment Banking | Africa-Focused

Overview

M. Lewis Capital Partners is an Africa-focused boutique investment firm that combines investment banking expertise with private equity capabilities to support SME capital raising, growth equity, and cross-border transactions across African markets.

We bridge the gap between African businesses seeking capital and international investors looking for credible, high-potential opportunities on the continent. Through deep local intelligence, trusted relationships, and market insight, we help investors navigate Africa's private markets with confidence.

We specialize in sub-USD 30 million transactions, with particular focus on founder-led businesses, SME expansion financing, and underserved mid-market opportunities across sectors including mining services, logistics and supply chain infrastructure, SME financial services, agribusiness, renewable energy, and infrastructure.
