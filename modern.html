About M. Lewis Group

M. Lewis Group is an Africa-focused investment platform and umbrella brand, uniting private equity, venture capital, and complementary sectors to unlock growth and impact across the continent. Our core divisions — M. Lewis Capital Partners and AfriLeap Capital — are supported by strategic initiatives in FinTech, ESG, real estate, and professional services to deliver end-to-end investment expertise.
