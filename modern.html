Who We Are

Strategic Advisory. Private Capital. Long-Term Partnership.

M. LEWIS GROUP is a strategic advisory and private capital platform focused on building, financing and scaling the businesses that will shape Africa's next economy.

We work with entrepreneurs, companies, investors and institutions at critical moments of growth, transformation and investment.

Our platform combines strategic advisory, venture advisory, corporate finance and access to private capital—giving clients more than advice through our three legal entities: Basiwel Ventures, AfriLeap Capital, and M. Lewis Capital Partners that serve entrepreneurs, privately held businesses, mid-market companies, established corporates, shareholders and investors across selected sectors of the Zambian and African economy.

Our Capabilities

Our Four Capabilities

Advice, relationships and capital—working together

📊

Strategic Advisory

We help leaders make better decisions, navigate complexity and create long-term enterprise value. From growth strategy and market expansion to transformation, restructuring and strategic partnerships, we work alongside management and boards on the decisions that matter most.

🚀

Venture Advisory

We work with ambitious founders and emerging businesses to build stronger, more scalable and investment-ready companies. We support strategy, commercialisation, governance, financial modelling, investor readiness, fundraising and growth.

📈

Corporate Finance Advisory

We advise businesses and shareholders on the transactions that shape value. Our capabilities include capital raising, mergers and acquisitions, valuation, financial structuring, restructuring, transaction preparation and strategic finance.

🤝

Capital Partners

We connect exceptional businesses and investment opportunities with appropriate sources of capital. Through our investor relationships, venture capabilities and private-capital platform, we seek to facilitate and, where appropriate, participate in transactions that create long-term value.

Our Approach

Strategy, Capital & Execution — Working Together

We believe the best outcomes are created when strategy, capital and execution work together.

That is why our role does not necessarily end when the advice is delivered.

We can help a business understand where it is going, prepare for the journey, structure the capital required to get there and connect it with the partners capable of accelerating its growth.

Where appropriate, we invest alongside the entrepreneurs and businesses we support.

Our Investment Perspective

Strengthening the Productive Economy

We focus on businesses that strengthen the productive economy.

We are particularly interested in opportunities that improve financial inclusion, increase productivity, strengthen supply chains, expand essential infrastructure and modernise commerce across Africa.

We believe strong economies are built by strong businesses.

Our objective is therefore not simply to finance businesses, but to help build enduring enterprises capable of creating employment, innovation, economic value and competitive advantage.
