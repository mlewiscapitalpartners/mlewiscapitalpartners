About Us

Building Africa’s Investment Future

M. Lewis Group is an Africa-focused investment and private capital platform uniting private equity, venture capital, ESG advisory, and complementary sectors to unlock growth, value, and impact across the continent.

Led by Founder & Managing Director, Mwaba Lewis, the Group combines hands-on consulting, investment execution, and strategic leadership to deliver high-impact solutions for corporates, SMEs, startups, development institutions, and public sector partners.

The Group also serves as a foundation for strategic expansion into education, FinTech, and sustainability solutions — ensuring long-term value creation across Africa.
